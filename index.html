Compress Audio for WhatsApp (Up to 3GB Input)

WhatsApp rejects attachments over 16MB. WaKit accepts source MP3, WAV, M4A, or AAC recordings up to 3GB, then transcodes them to 64k (voice), 96k, or 128k MP3/OPUS locally — cutting size by up to 70% while keeping speech clear. Large files are mounted read-only from disk instead of being copied into browser memory first.

How to use

  1. Step 1: Drop an audio file up to 3GB into the compressor.
  2. Step 2: Choose bitrate (64k for voice) and MP3 or OPUS output.
  3. Step 3: Compress and download the file, ready to send on WhatsApp.

Why client-side WebAssembly is safer than cloud-upload converters

Cloud converters require uploading personal videos, voice notes, and phone numbers to remote servers where they may be stored, scanned, or leaked. WaKit instead runs FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly inside your browser's sandbox: bytes are processed in local memory and never transmitted. No accounts, no logs, no retention.
