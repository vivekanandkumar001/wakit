Why client-side WebAssembly is safer than cloud-upload converters

Cloud converters require uploading personal videos, voice notes, and phone numbers to remote servers where they may be stored, scanned, or leaked. WhatsSwift instead runs FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly inside your browser's sandbox: bytes are processed in local memory and never transmitted. No accounts, no logs, no retention.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All processing runs as WebAssembly inside your browser. Files never leave your device.

Why 30 seconds per status clip?

WhatsApp enforces a 30-second cap per status story. The splitter cuts exact 30s segments so each uploads cleanly.

How do I stay under the 16MB audio limit?

Compress to 64 kbps for voice notes. A 50MB lecture shrinks to roughly 10–14MB and sends instantly.

Is direct chat without saving a number safe?

Yes. It only opens WhatsApp's official wa.me link. No contact is saved and nothing is stored anywhere.
